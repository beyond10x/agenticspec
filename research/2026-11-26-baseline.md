# Baseline: Claude Code sessions on the owner's workstation, 72 hours

Window: 2026-11-23 00:08 UTC to 2026-11-26 00:08 UTC (01:08 CET to 01:08 CET).
Source: every `*.jsonl` under `~/.claude/projects` modified in the window, records with a
timestamp in the window. 3,309 files: 106 main sessions, 3,201 sub-agent transcripts.

Measured with a temporary script (session scratch directory, not kept; workspace rule "No Python
for permanent tools"). The numbers below are what this repository's first KPI implementation must
reproduce or explain.

## Cache

- Hit ratio (read / (read + write + uncached input)): main 98.4 %, sub-agents 97.7 %.
- Cache read tokens: main 13.5 B, sub-agents 48.5 B. Cache write: main 214 M, sub-agents 1.12 B.
- Cache bust: a message after a context above 20k that reads under half of the previous context
  from cache and writes more than half of it. Cause, derived:

| scope | cause | busts | tokens written again |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| main | gap longer than the cache TTL | 185 | 40.4 M |
| main | model changed | 24 | 3.8 M |
| main | neither (unexplained) | 47 | 9.3 M |
| sub | gap longer than the cache TTL | 2,204 | 386.9 M |
| sub | neither (unexplained) | 191 | 16.0 M |
| sub | model changed | 2 | 0.4 M |

Hypothesis, not checked: sub-agent TTL busts come from waits longer than 5 minutes (build queue,
long tool calls) on a 5-minute cache.

## Concurrency

An actor is active in a minute when two of its records are at most 5 minutes apart around it.

- Main sessions active at once: p50 8, p90 14, max 52 (peak hour 2026-11-25 15 UTC).
- Sub-agents active at once: p50 24, p90 51, max 67.
- All actors: p50 34, p90 60, max 87.

## Autonomy

Every main session is `sessionKind: bg` except 11 with no kind recorded.

- Prompts counted as human: 1,064. At least 531 of them are not typed by the owner: briefs handed to a
  headless session ("Your brief is the file …", 118) and text typed by a session that acts for the
  owner ("Typed by the session <name> …", 30), counted from the 25 most frequent openings only.
- The transcript has no field that tells the owner's prompt from a prompt another session typed. The
  only marks are text prefixes. Queued prompts carry `origin.kind` (`human` 550,
  `task-notification` 5,898, `peer`), direct prompts do not.
- 43 of 106 main sessions have at most one human-counted prompt.
- Wall time with the agent's turn ended and the next input a human-counted prompt: 17.6 h in gaps
  up to 1 h, 84.7 h in longer gaps. Agent-active time: 369 h. These are upper bounds for
  "waiting on the owner" because of the attribution gap above.
- Permission decisions on tool results of main sessions: 74,426 by mode or classifier
  (`source: config`), 236 by the owner once (`user_temporary`), 212 by a hook, 262 with none.
  Time from tool call to result for non-config decisions: 25.7 h summed, including the tool's own
  run time.

Across all 3,309 files (main and sub): accept by mode 352,166, accept by classifier 119,899,
reject by hook 2,588, accept by the owner once 345, reject by classifier 112, reject by safety check
27.
