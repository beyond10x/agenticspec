description: "One planner agent writes a plan for every task and posts it to a queue; executor agents, woken by each post, take the plans in order. With a plan before the first change, resolved rose from 50.2% to 65.0% while the planner used under 2% of the executor's tokens (2610.29870); a plan preceded every first change in 23 of 23 runs (2610.35992). The simulator gives the planner the profile role `planner` (one turn per task) and the executors the role `executor`, whose pass rate after a failed step is set so that the share of correct tasks rises by the ratio of 2610.29870: the gain of a plan is an input of the run, not a result of it. A post that finds every executor busy is offered again when a run ends. The simulator does not model a wrong plan, an executor that departs from its plan, plans that depend on each other, nor a planner that learns from failed runs."
