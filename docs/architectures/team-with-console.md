## Links

No link, no reach: a part reaches only what a link names.

- **owner** sends `prompt` to **console.steward**.
- **console.steward** sends `question` to **owner**.
- **console.steward** sends `order` to **worker**.
- **worker** sends `question` to **console.steward**.
- **worker** sends `model_call` to **gateway**.
- **gateway** sends `model_call` to **model**.
