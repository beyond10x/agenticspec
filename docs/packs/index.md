Three tiers, each in its own directories, with dependencies pointing down only:

- the [core](../blocks/index.md) (`blocks/`): what every agentic system has, whatever its work;
- packs (`packs/<pack>/`): concepts more than one field reuses; a pack depends on the core and on the packs its `pack.yaml` lists under `uses:`;
- [fields](../fields/index.md) (`fields/<field>/`): one kind of work; a field lists the packs it uses and adds a block only for a concept no other field shares.

`task check:layers` refuses a core block that depends on a pack or a field, a pack that depends on a field or on a pack it does not list, and a field that depends on another field. The whole system validates from the root `ess-inputs.yaml`; the core still validates alone from `blocks/ess-inputs.yaml`.
