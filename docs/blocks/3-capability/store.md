Data a part writes and another part reads. A store has one of five kinds (record, artifact, key-value, semantic, graph), a scope and a retention. A record is one entry of a store under a key: it is put, confirmed by the service that plays the store, and stays present until a newer revision supersedes it, it expires or it is deleted. The block records that a write happened, its key, the digest of its content and who wrote it, never the bytes; the bytes live behind the store's port. An artifact is a record of an artifact store: its content is never revised, its key is its digest, and its provenance (who recorded it, which artifacts it derives from) is required. Reads are recorded too: a get by key and a query, each with the digests of what was asked and answered and a count. A store names no part: the architecture names the store a part links to.

### Record

One entry of a store under a key. It is Writing from the put until the service that plays the store confirms or fails it. A confirmed record is Present until a newer revision under its key supersedes it, it expires or it is deleted. A record of an artifact store is an artifact: its key is the digest of its content, and no revision supersedes it.

Identified by `record_id` (`RecordId`).

| field | type | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `store_id` | `StoreId` |  |
| `kind` | `StoreKind` | The kind of its store, kept on the record so a guard on it needs no second read. |
| `key` | `string` | The key it is stored under; for an artifact the digest of its content. |
| `revision` | `integer` | 1 for the first record under a key, one more for each record that supersedes the one before. |
| `content` | `Content` | Digest, length and class of the content; for an artifact the class is a row a field registers. |
| `recorded_by` | `PrincipalId` | Who put it; an agent is a principal of kind Agent. |
| `recorded_at` | `timestamp` |  |

| `supersedes` | `optional RecordId` | The record under the same key that it replaces when confirmed; absent for the first record under a key. |
| `expires_at` | `optional timestamp` | When the clock of the store expires it. The writer sets it from the store's retention: the time of the put plus its seconds for a Duration retention, none for Forever; a Run retention ends the record with the instance of the architecture. |
