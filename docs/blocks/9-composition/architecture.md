An architecture composes building blocks into one concrete setup: parts (named instances of a block, with a count), links (which part may call or message which, and what the link carries), a border (the parts a human principal reaches) and immutable revisions. No link, no reach: two parts without a link cannot reach each other.

## Entities

### Architecture

One composed setup, edited as parts, links and a border, frozen by each commit into a revision.

Identified by `architecture_id` (`ArchitectureId`).
