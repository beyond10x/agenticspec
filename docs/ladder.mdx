## The model

**Throughput** is the amount of work that runs through a person per unit of time, as opposed to the
work that person does by hand. **Trust** is that person's ability to establish that the output is
correct without reading every line.

The ladder has seven stages. Each stage is a band of throughput with one **marker**, what is
observably true at that stage, and one **trust need**, what must be in place for that throughput to
be safe. **The rule:** each stage of throughput needs its matching trust. Throughput without it
produces bad output faster.

<StageTable />

A **barrier** is a change of mental model, not of tooling. It sits between two stages, and it is
where people stall. It is crossed only by what someone does on the far side, never by talking about
it.

<BarrierTable />

## The rungs

The animation follows one owner's journey: a sandboxed session at a workstation, the sandbox
switched off, sub-agents, background sessions, scheduled agents, experiments, a swarm, a live loop
and finally a factory of teams. Each rung is an architecture document made of the
[building blocks](/docs/blocks), and `agenticspec-sim check` accepts every one of them. A part keeps
its name from rung to rung, so the picture grows instead of being redrawn.

<RungTable />

The ladder itself, `ladder/ladder.yaml`, is checked too. Every stage has a rung, every barrier sits
between two neighbouring stages, a part keeps its block across rungs, and every trust part exists in
its rung's architecture. A rung that raises the number of agent copies at work, or drops a trust
part, without adding a trust part is refused unless it names the risk it takes. The second rung, the
sandbox switched off, is the one that names it.

To regenerate the data of this page after changing the ladder or a rung:

```sh
task ladder:generate   # agenticspec-sim ladder ladder/ladder.yaml --out website/static/ladder/ladder.json
task check:ladder      # fails when the data file is stale
```
