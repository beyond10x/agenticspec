## Outcome

Every architecture page (`docs/architectures/<name>.md`, 10 pages) and every catalog entry
(`docs/catalog/<entry>.md`, 6 entries) of the docs site has a section "Contract": the ports the
architecture uses (port, kind, block, protocol, part, narrowing settings, instances), its routing
invariants and its border, and a link to download the contract file. The pages stay generated by
`task docs:generate` and held by `task check:docs`.

## Scope

- `crates/agenticspec-docs/src/arch.rs` (`page`): a "Contract" section after "Check", drawn from
  `agenticspec-sim`'s contract derivation (`story:contract-derivation`, called as a library, no
  subprocess) as a Markdown table with one row per port (port, kind, block, protocol, part,
  narrowing settings, instances), a list of invariants and the border; a refused architecture
  shows no contract and says why.
- `crates/agenticspec-docs/src/catalog.rs` (`page`, `files`, the downloads list): the same section
  on each entry, and `<entry>.contract.yaml` among the downloads, the contract of the
  architecture as written (the best variant's contract is not in this story).
- `crates/agenticspec-docs/src/lib.rs`, `src/main.rs`: write `website/static/architectures/<name>.contract.yaml`
  and `website/static/catalog/<entry>/<entry>.contract.yaml`; `--check` covers them.
- `crates/agenticspec-docs/tests/main.rs`: one docs test over all 10 architecture pages and all 6
  catalog entries, on the pages `generate` returns (one generation for the test, as the existing
  tests do; the derivation reads the committed `blocks/ports.json` and calls no `ess`). Per page:
  a "Contract" section; one table row per port of that document's contract, with the port's
  protocol; the border; the download link. Per catalog entry: `<entry>.contract.yaml` in the
  downloads list and among the generated files, its bytes equal to the derived contract. The test
  asserts the counts (10 pages, 6 entries), so a page without the section fails it. Under 5 s.
- Generated output: `docs/architectures/`, `docs/catalog/`, `website/static/architectures/`,
  `website/static/catalog/`, `website/src/generated/` (`task docs:generate`).
- `docs/contract.md`: a link to the sections; `CHANGELOG.md`.
- `task check:site` (`slot task check:site`) on the built site: the new section passes the layout,
  axe and keyboard gates and the ratchet in `ux/site/baseline.json` does not get worse.
- Builds and tests through `slot` (`task docs:generate` as `slot --here`); no test over 60 s, the
  step within 120 s, a pool job over 10 minutes is killed. No full experiment runs
  (`task docs:generate` never runs one).

## Shared surfaces

- The contract format and `crates/agenticspec-sim/src/contract/`: `story:contract-derivation`
  (comes first); `story:routing-invariants` changes the simulator after this story.
- `website/static/catalog/`: `story:routing-invariants` regenerates the catalog results there
  (`sources.sha256`).
- `crates/agenticspec-docs/tests/`: `story:port-derivation` (its digest test, comes first).
- `docs/contract.md`: `story:port-derivation`, `story:contract-derivation`,
  `story:contract-conformance`, `story:routing-invariants`.
- `docs/blocks/`, `website/src/generated/`: `story:block-bindings` regenerates them.
- Outside this epic, `story:architecture-kpis` adds sections to the same pages
  (`crates/agenticspec-docs`, `docs/architectures`, `docs/catalog`, `website/static/catalog`,
  `website/src/generated`).
- `CHANGELOG.md`: every story of this epic.

## Acceptance

- Given the 10 architecture documents and the 6 catalog entries, when the docs test of
  `crates/agenticspec-docs/tests/main.rs` runs, it passes: each of the 10 pages
  `docs/architectures/<name>.md` and each of the 6 entries `docs/catalog/<entry>.md` has a
  "Contract" section with one row per port of `agenticspec sim contract` on its document, each row
  with that port's protocol, and its border; each entry's downloads list `<entry>.contract.yaml`,
  equal byte for byte to that contract.
- Given `architectures/workstation.yaml`, when `task docs:generate` runs,
  `docs/architectures/workstation.md` lists every port of its contract (8 unless
  `story:port-derivation` left one to an ESS gap) and not the workload, clock or board port, with
  part `tree` at 12 instances and the border `[operator]`, and
  `website/static/architectures/workstation.contract.yaml` is byte for byte the output of
  `agenticspec sim contract architectures/workstation.yaml`.
- Given `task docs:generate` done, when `ls website/static/architectures/*.contract.yaml
  website/static/catalog/*/*.contract.yaml | wc -l` runs, it prints 16.
- Given the generated pages, when one contract file is edited by hand, `task check:docs` exits 1
  naming it; after `task docs:generate` it exits 0, and `slot task check:site` exits 0.

## Not in this story

A contract on the ladder page; the contract of a catalog entry's best variant; filtering
conformance (`story:contract-conformance`); invariants on a trace (`story:routing-invariants`).
