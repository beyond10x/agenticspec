Architecture

handoff

A sequential hand-off: agent a works first and hands its result to agent b. In 2611.00314 this solved 54% at the cost of one agent; more alternating turns gave no gain at 1.59 times the cost. The simulator has no hand-off: the `handoff` link is drawn and checked but not simulated, so a and b both take tasks from the backlog as two independent workers.
