Architecture

console

A console as an architecture of its own, made to be nested in another one: a steward agent is the one part on its border, hands work to a helper agent, and both reach their model through a gateway. A document that nests it reaches the steward as `console.steward` and nothing else of it. On its own the console has no principal: it is checked and drawn here, and simulated inside the architecture that nests it, team-with-console.
