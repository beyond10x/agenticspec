The task "read the best variant of the search" ends in the Marked places table. On
author-reviewer it starts at 4272 px at 1440x900: the screen-only user scrolls 6 times at
1440x900 and 7 times at 1280x800, and its median look finds the target out of view (glance 3).
The architecture pages hide the table of contents, so neither the right-hand column nor an
"On this page" list offers Check, Parts, Links, Border, Policies, Marked places or Optimization.
The 5 architecture pages share the layout; the cost grows with the frame's height. It is the
worst task of the round on the ratchet (actions 9 and 10 against 2 to 6 for the others).
Sessions: best-variant-of-author-reviewer, all 4 gating runs and the 2 runs at 1024x768.

Caveat: in S-01 the frame draws the landing page (SUF-001), so its 2052 px is the landing page's
height. With the architecture in the frame the height changes; the missing contents list does not.

Fix in one line: show the page's contents (or a short list of jump links) above the frame, or put
the result tables before it.

Fix (S-01 change set, closed in S-02): crates/agenticspec-docs writes the architecture page with its contents list (no `hide_table_of_contents`) and, for an architecture with an experiment, the search result first: "Search result" (variants tried, fitness as built and of the best variant), "Marked places" with the best value of each place, "KPIs that moved" (each KPI whose value differs, the ones the experiment weighs first, with the direction of the change), then "Graph" with the frame.
