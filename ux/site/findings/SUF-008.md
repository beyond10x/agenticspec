The only axe-core serious violation of the round. At 390x844 a lifecycle of harness (488 px)
scrolls inside a 356 px box; the box holds no focusable element, so a keyboard user cannot scroll
it to the states on its right. The same box scrolls on 8 block pages on a
phone and on the work page at 1024x768, where the same rule would apply (not scanned there). Not gated: at both gating
viewports the diagrams fit and axe-core reports nothing. S2 because it is a WCAG 2.1.1 failure,
although at a measured size only.

Fix in one line: give the scrolling frame tabIndex 0, a role of region and the entity's name as
its label, or make the diagram fit the column.

Fix (S-01 change set, closed in S-02): website/src/components/blocks/scrollRegion.ts gives a box that scrolls sideways tabindex 0, role region and a name while its content is wider than the box, and nothing while it fits (the rule of the docs-system's tables). Used by the lifecycle diagram ("Lifecycle of <entity>"), the KPI table ("KPI table") and the layer map ("Layer map"). The embedded architecture page does the same for its tables (crates/agenticspec-render/assets/page.js, a change the S-02 build made before that crate changed hands). Red before: axe scrollable-region-focusable on figure > .as-life__frame of harness at 390x844. Green after: 0, and no box in main that scrolls sideways without a focusable named region.
