The Checks card says "Cache bust after idle  Found · 1" and "Human correction  Unknown · 1 (1
possible)". The numbers have no noun (1 what: turn, event, match?), "1 possible" repeats the word
it should explain, and "Unknown" does not say whether the check could not decide or found nothing.
The card head says "1 found", and the scenario card, in the reader's words, asks for "the check it
failed": three words for one state. The failing-check runs end right, so the cost is a second read
beside the found check, not a wrong answer. Sessions: failing-check, agent-lane, embed-session:
the list is on every session and embed page, and 30 of 50 runs open one. Cost: not estimated.

Fix in one line: name what the count counts and say an unknown verdict in words ("could not decide: 1 possible match").
